What we process

Operational Inbox processes account details, connected domain configuration, inbound and outbound email content, attachments, delivery events, and audit records so it can provide the service. When you connect an agent, the agent can access only the data and actions you authorize through the displayed OAuth scopes.

How we use data

We use this data to receive, organize, search, draft, send, secure, support, and improve Operational Inbox. We do not sell personal data. Email content returned through agent tools is limited to the connected user’s authorized domains.

Service providers and retention

We use infrastructure and service providers for hosting, email delivery, storage, billing, security, and AI-assisted features. Data is retained according to account settings, legal obligations, security requirements, and backup cycles. Revoking an agent connection prevents new access but does not delete mailbox data.

Your choices

You can revoke API tokens and agent connections, update retention settings, or request account and data support. Contact support@operationalinbox.com for access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions.
